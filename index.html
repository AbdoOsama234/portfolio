About Me

Who I Am

A brief introduction about my journey as a developer

I'm a Mid-Level Flutter Engineer with a passion for building user-centric mobile applications. With a BSc in Software Development from New Cairo Technological University and 3+ years of hands-on experience, I specialize in creating scalable, high-performance cross-platform apps.

I've had the privilege of working with companies like CodeWFekra and Crystal Consulting Services, where I designed and developed multiple mobile applications across industries including HR Management, Ride-Hailing, Food Delivery, Recruitment, Real Estate, and Car Marketplace platforms.

My approach combines Clean Architecture principles with modern design patterns like BLoC to deliver code that is maintainable, testable, and delightful to use. I'm always exploring new technologies and pushing the boundaries of what's possible with Flutter.
